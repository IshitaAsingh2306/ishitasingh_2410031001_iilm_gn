## 🔄 Design Workflow

The design process followed these general stages:

1.  Understand the project brief and visual requirements
2.  Develop an appropriate creative direction
3.  Convert the concept into a visual design
4.  Share the design with the relevant team
5.  Review and incorporate feedback
6.  Finalize and deliver the design
